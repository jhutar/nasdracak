# Introduction

For nine years the bell in the chapel of Mire's Hollow has not rung. The
people of the village forgot its voice. Tonight it rang thirteen times, and
every door in the village stood open.

::: readaloud
The bell in the old chapel has not rung in nine years. Tonight it rang
thirteen times. The villagers do not speak of it. They do not sleep.
:::

# The Village of Mire's Hollow

The village is small: a mill, a well, a tavern called The Drowned Bell, and
the chapel on the hill. The villagers are wary of visitors. The tavern keeper
will share this much for 5 gp:

::: squarebox
**A Riddle on the Chapel Door**
*No key turns in my lock of bone,*
*yet what is buried chimes its song.*
*Count the tolling, threefold known —*
*thirteen wake the dead and wrong.*
:::

## The Shuttered Chapel

The chapel door is sealed with iron bands. A DC 12 Strength check (Athletics)
or a holy symbol of the village faith breaks the seal. Inside: dust, a
collapsed bell rope, and one item of value in the vestry.

::: dnditem
Bell of the Hollow Choir
Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)
While attuned, you can cast *speak with dead* at will. Once per day you may
ring the bell; its toll is heard within 60 feet and all undead within that
range must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be *frightened* until
dawn.
:::

# Encounter: The Bell Keepers

Behind the chapel, a collapsed stairway leads down. At its foot stand two
skeletons in rusted vestments, each holding a rusted scythe. They do not
attack until a character rings the chapel bell or passes within 10 feet.

::: dndmonster
name: Skeleton Bellkeeper
type: Medium undead, neutral evil
armor_class: 12 (bone plating)
hit_points: 13 (2d8 + 2)
speed: 30 ft.
str: 10
dex: 12
con: 10
int: 6
wis: 8
cha: 5
senses: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9
languages: understands Common, but cannot speak
challenge: 1/4

---

**Actions**

**Rusty Scythe.** *Melee Weapon Attack.* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. **Hit.** 6 (1d6 + 1) slashing damage.

**Toll.** The bellkeeper strikes a small hand bell. One creature of the
bellkeeper's choice within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution
saving throw or be **frightened** until the end of its next turn.
:::

::: readaloud
As the second bellkeeper falls, it reaches up and mimes the tolling of a
great bell. Thirteen times. Then it is still.
:::
